feat: training/match modes, and an archive grouped by season

Two features, both driven by the same observation: the app had grown into one
long menu where a coach at the touchline had to scroll past subscriptions to
reach the lineup, and last season's numbers were indistinguishable from this
season's.

Modes

Two big targets at the top of the sidebar, FC-menu style, replacing the flat
menu:

- Training — overview, players, schedule, attendance, finance
- Match — match centre only (XI, clock, result)

The lineup page's own "training mode / official match" toggle is gone. It only
ever toggled the match timer's visibility, so it was a second, contradictory
place to express the same choice the new top-level mode now makes.

The mode is persisted to localStorage and read via useSyncExternalStore rather
than useState + useEffect, so the correct menu renders on the first paint and
two open tabs stay in step.

Archive

Read-only history grouped by season. Nothing is deleted or moved: a coach ends
a season by creating the next season's age group in Settings, and the old rows
stay under the old heading.

Season is derived from the owning team's `season` string rather than stored on
sessions and lineups, which keeps it consistent with how groups are already
named. Shows sessions (with attendance counts) and matches (with results), with
a season picker, a text search, per-season W-D-L and goal totals, and toggles to
show one kind or the other.

Two counting rules worth keeping, both verified by assertions against
lib/offline/archive.ts:

- a lineup with only one of goals_for/goals_against is a half-entered result and
  is listed but excluded from the record; counting it would silently invent 0-0
  draws
- a session with no attendance rows reads as "never marked", not "0 present of
  0", which would imply everyone turned up
