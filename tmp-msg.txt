refactor: strip Settings down to what a coach actually uses

The panel had grown to seven sections, three of which were either developer
scaffolding or actively dangerous sitting next to everyday controls.

Removed:

- "Empty sessions" cleanup. It repaired a bug where opening the attendance page
  silently created a session every day. That bug is fixed, so the tool is a
  one-time migration with no audience left. findProbablyEmptySessions(),
  deleteSessions() and localDataStats() went with it.
- Separate "push to cloud" / "pull from cloud" buttons. They invited the wrong
  one -- push-only is precisely what made a second device look empty while the
  data sat in Supabase. One "sync now" button does both, in the correct order.
- A raw row-count readout (clubs: 1, players: 18, sessions: 42...). Debug
  output, not information a coach acts on.
- "Reset" as a top-level red section, with "load demo data" beside it. It is
  now collapsed under "advanced tools", because a wipe-everything button at the
  same visual weight as "add a club" is a misclick waiting to happen. Deleting
  the cloud account remains the visible way out.

Reworded the sync description to state what actually happens now (automatic,
both directions) rather than advertising manual push and pull.

No data or behaviour change to anything a coach relies on.
