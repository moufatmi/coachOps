fix: printing a player card no longer prints the whole squad

Printing a player's profile produced that player's card followed by every other
player card on the page.

The profile modal deliberately becomes `print:static` so its printable sheet
lands in the output, but the squad grid it sits on top of had no print rule of
its own. Only `aside`, `header` and `nav` are hidden by the global print styles,
so the directory rendered underneath stayed visible.

Wrapped the directory (header, search box and card grid) in `no-print`. The modal
is rendered outside that wrapper, so the printable sheet is untouched.

Also added the player's photo to the printed card, since photos now exist and a
printed card without the face is a smaller win than it could be.
